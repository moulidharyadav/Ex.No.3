# EX-03 – Applying Different Prompting Techniques to an AI Use Case

## AIM

To create prompts using Straightforward Prompting, Tabular Format Prompting, Preceding Question Prompting, and Missing Word Prompting, and evaluate their effectiveness using a rating method.

## SELECTED USE CASE

**AI in Education – Personalized Learning**

Personalized learning uses Artificial Intelligence to understand students' learning needs and provide suitable study materials, explanations, and practice activities.

## PROCEDURE

### 1. Straightforward Prompting

**Prompt:**

"Explain how Artificial Intelligence helps students in personalized learning in simple English."

**Output:**

Artificial Intelligence helps students learn according to their individual needs. It identifies their strengths and weaknesses and provides suitable lessons, practice questions, and feedback. This makes learning easier and more effective.

### 2. Tabular Format Prompting

**Prompt:**

"Compare traditional learning and AI-based personalized learning in a table with five differences."

**Output:**

| Traditional Learning | AI-Based Personalized Learning |
|---|---|
| Same lessons for all students | Lessons based on individual needs |
| Teacher provides most feedback | AI provides instant feedback |
| Fixed learning speed | Flexible learning speed |
| Limited personal guidance | Personalized recommendations |
| Mostly classroom-based | Can support online learning |

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting

**Prompt:**

"What difficulties do students face in traditional learning? How can Artificial Intelligence solve these difficulties through personalized learning?"

**Output:**

Students may face difficulties such as different learning speeds, lack of individual attention, and difficulty understanding certain topics.

Artificial Intelligence can help by identifying students' learning difficulties, providing personalized explanations, recommending practice exercises, and giving immediate feedback. Therefore, AI can support students in improving their learning experience.

### 4. Missing Word Prompting

**Prompt:**

"Artificial Intelligence in personalized learning helps students by identifying their strengths and ______, providing suitable study materials, and giving instant ______."

**Output:**

1. Weaknesses
2. Feedback

**Completed Sentence:**

Artificial Intelligence in personalized learning helps students by identifying their strengths and weaknesses, providing suitable study materials, and giving instant feedback.

## EVALUATION METHOD

The four prompting techniques are evaluated using a 5-point rating scale based on the following criteria:

- Accuracy
- Clarity
- Relevance
- Completeness

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

## RESULT

The four prompting techniques were successfully created and applied to the selected use case, AI in Education – Personalized Learning. Their outputs were evaluated using accuracy, clarity, relevance, and completeness.

The evaluation showed that Tabular Format Prompting and Preceding Question Prompting provided more structured and detailed responses. Straightforward Prompting produced a simple and direct explanation, while Missing Word Prompting was useful for completing sentences and testing basic knowledge.

## CONCLUSION

Thus, the experiment helped to understand and apply four different prompting techniques. Each technique has its own purpose and produces different types of responses. Tabular Format Prompting is useful for comparisons, Preceding Question Prompting provides detailed explanations, Straightforward Prompting gives direct answers, and Missing Word Prompting is useful for filling in missing information. Selecting the appropriate prompting technique helps improve the quality and usefulness of AI-generated responses.
